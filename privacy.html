DetailDeck

Privacy Policy

DetailDeck is designed as a local-first business app. Customer records, job notes, pricing details, and selected job photos are stored on the user's device unless the user chooses to export or share them through iOS.

Data Collection

The MVP does not require an account and does not send customer or job data to a company server.

Permissions

Camera and photo library access are used only to attach job photos. Notification permission may be used for local follow-up reminders.

Contact

Questions can be sent to FreeRangeLabs@drumnbus.com.
